Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Baton lets you view and continue the coding-agent Sessions running on your own computer from your other devices. We designed it so that we cannot read your conversations.

Who we are

Baton (“we”) operates the Baton apps, the Bridge, and the hosted relay service at agentbaton.app. Questions or requests: support@agentbaton.app.

What we store

  • Account and device records: your account ID, the names and types of the devices you connect, hashed access tokens, push notification tokens, and encrypted keys that we cannot open.
  • Your Sessions stay private: Session content and messages are end-to-end encrypted between your devices and your computer. They are not stored on our servers and we cannot read them.
  • Files you transfer (for example images you attach or files you open): other files up to 50 MB are stored encrypted. Videos, audio and any file over 50 MB are stored unencrypted in a private area that only your account can access. All transferred files are automatically deleted after 1 day.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, they are delivered through Apple Push Notification service. Their content is encrypted, and your device decrypts it.

Usage statistics

To run the service, we keep anonymous, aggregate statistics: daily active accounts, connection time, platform, and country. We do not use advertising, we do not track you across apps or websites, we do not sell data, and we use no third-party analytics.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare: hosting for the website and relay service; it processes network data such as IP addresses to deliver them.
  • Apple: push notification delivery.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove devices and delete your account in the App. Deleting your account removes the data we store for it. You can also contact us to request deletion.

Children

Baton is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and revise the effective date above.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

Baton 让你在其他设备上查看并继续使用自己电脑上运行的编程 Agent Session。我们在设计上就无法读取你的对话内容。

我们是谁

Baton（“我们”）负责运营 Baton App、Bridge 以及 agentbaton.app 上的托管中转服务。如有疑问或请求，请联系 support@agentbaton.app。

我们保存哪些数据

  • 账户与设备记录：账户 ID、你所连接设备的名称和类型、经过哈希处理的访问令牌、推送通知令牌，以及我们无法解开的加密密钥。
  • Session 内容只有你能看到：Session 内容和消息在你的设备与电脑之间端到端加密，不会保存在我们的服务器上，我们也无法读取。
  • 你传输的文件（例如附加的图片或打开的文件）：视频、音频以外、50 MB 以内的文件加密保存；视频、音频以及超过 50 MB 的文件不加密，存放在只有你的账户能访问的私有区域。所有传输的文件会在 1 天后自动删除。

通知

开启通知后，通知通过 Apple 推送通知服务（APNs）送达，内容经过加密，由你的设备解密显示。

使用统计

为了运营服务，我们只保留匿名的汇总统计：每日活跃账户数、连接时长、平台和国家/地区。我们不投放广告，不跨应用或网站追踪你，不出售任何数据，也不使用第三方分析服务。

服务提供商

  • Cloudflare：托管网站和中转服务，并为此处理 IP 地址等网络数据。
  • Apple：推送通知投递。

你的选择与数据删除

你可以在 App 中移除设备或删除账户。删除账户后，我们为该账户保存的数据也会一并删除。你也可以联系我们申请删除。

儿童

Baton 是面向开发者的工具，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的数据。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。
